# FoodHub / Cada pedido cuenta
## Style prompt
A monochrome cinema trailer for a mobile point of sale. Oversized confident typography, luminous device edges, real grouped white UI, sparse chapter labels and measured camera depth. Product clarity governs every frame.
## Colors
- Canvas #000000
- Foreground #FFFFFF
- App surface #F2F2F7
- App text / primary buttons #000000
- Outer secondary text #B8B8C1; inner secondary text #606068 for video contrast
## Typography
System sans throughout the product and titles, faithful to source. Monospace chapter labels. Large 110–150px trailer headings, 60px brand, 26–34px product body.
## Do not
No neon, unrelated gradients, stock photos, generic SaaS claims or fake business performance. No live customer or account data. Preserve black primary actions and white grouped cards. No narration.

## Reel adaptation
1080×1920, 20 seconds. Stack copy above device; keep content away from right controls and lower captions. Source Lucide icons are inline SVG with currentColor so the selected black card shows white symbols. Landscape original preserved in ../composition-landscape/.

## Product imagery
Cafe and sandwich use locally bundled generated food photographs, natural soft daylight and warm neutral stone background. Labels removed from every on-screen scene per user request; typography and existing timing preserved.
