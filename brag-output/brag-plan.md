# Brag Plan: FoodHub

## The nine answers
1. FoodHub is a mobile POS for registering sales and viewing daily sales and orders.
2. Strongest grounded moment: “Venta registrada” after a payment confirmation.
3. Visual hook: enormous white “Cada pedido cuenta.” against black, with a luminous phone emerging.
4. Recreate source UI: product catalog → Total a cobrar / Medio de pago → Venta registrada, plus Vendido hoy dashboard.
5. 20 seconds is the shortest satisfying complete product story here.
6. Cinematic: a restrained monochrome trailer for the work behind every order.
7. Low steady music, two weighted reveal hits and precise UI clicks; no narration.
8. “Cada pedido cuenta. FoodHub: productos, cobro y ventas del día, desde el móvil.”
9. Choose products → confirm payment → registered sale; daily summary gives context.

## Angle and visual identity
An everyday sale gets the scale of a movie premiere. Keep UI faithful to source, with black primary pills, white grouped cards and #F2F2F7 screen surfaces. Outer canvas #000000, primary text #FFFFFF, secondary #B8B8C1. System sans matches the app; monospace labels provide quiet chapter numbering. No invented speed, growth, availability or performance claims. All business, product, order and revenue values are fictional demo content, explicitly labelled “Datos de demostración”. No live account data is used.

## Format and duration
Landscape 1920×1080, 20 seconds. Music and SFX enabled. Voice disabled.

## Storyboard
1. 0–3.2s / Hook: “Cada pedido cuenta.”, FoodHub small wordmark. Large type enters with weight, phone silhouette in depth. No interaction. Soft opening impact. Dramatic reveal into scene 2.
2. 3.2–8.74s / El pedido: source-inspired POS catalog with fictional Café $2.500 and Sandwich $5.000, source-style quick-add buttons. Two simulated taps select one of each; the bottom “Ver pedido · $7.500” appears. Copy “Elige. Agrega.” Main UI holds 2 seconds after selection. Restrained card arrivals, two clicks. Smooth transition.
3. 8.74–13.11s / El cobro: source checkout UI, Total a cobrar $7.500, 2 productos, Efectivo / Tarjeta / Transferencia. A tap selects Tarjeta, then “Confirmar pago · $7.500”. Text “Confirma el pago.” Holds a readable checkout before transition. Two quiet clicks.
4. 13.11–17.47s / Resultado: “Venta registrada”, $7.500, Pedido #1042, Nueva venta. Adjacent daily summary uses source labels “Vendido hoy”, “Pedidos”, “Ticket promedio”. Revenue $75.000, 10 orders, $7.500 average. Big reveal hit. No implied backend live operation; this is a source-grounded UI recreation. Crossfade to title.
5. 17.47–20s / Outro: “FoodHub” with “Cada pedido cuenta.” and “POS · Pedidos · Resumen”. A quiet final bell, music fades to finish. Hold lockup at least 1.5 seconds.
